# Refund Policy

This article explains when and how you receive money back after a return or a cancelled order.

## Refund timeline

1. **Inspection:** we process the refund within **5 business days** after the returned item arrives at our warehouse.
2. **Bank posting:** after we issue the refund, your bank or card issuer usually takes **3 to 10 business days** to show it on your statement.

You receive an email when the refund is issued. If 10 business days have passed since that email and you still cannot see the refund, contact support with your order number.

## Refund method

Refunds always go to the **original payment method**. We cannot redirect a refund to a different card or account.

## Partial refunds

The refund may be lower than the amount paid when:

- a restocking fee applies (opened laptops, tablets, monitors and drones, 15%);
- the return was not due to a defect or a wrong item, in which case the $6.95 return shipping fee is deducted;
- accessories or parts are missing from the returned package.

## Cancelled orders

If you cancel an order before it ships, no charge is captured. Any pending authorization on your card is released by your bank, usually within 3 to 5 business days (see "Double Charges and Pending Authorizations").

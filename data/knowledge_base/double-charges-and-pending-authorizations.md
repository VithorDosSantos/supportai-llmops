# Double Charges and Pending Authorizations

Seeing two transactions for one order is usually caused by a **pending authorization**, not by a real double charge.

## Pending authorization vs. charge

- When you place an order, we place a temporary **authorization hold** for the order amount.
- When the order ships, we **capture** the payment. Some banks briefly show both the hold and the final charge.
- If you edit or cancel an order, a new hold may be created. The old hold is released by your bank, usually within **3 to 5 business days**.

Holds are not charges: the money never leaves your account and the hold disappears on its own.

## If you were really charged twice

If two **posted** (not pending) transactions appear for the same order:

1. Check in **Account > Orders** that you did not place two orders.
2. Contact Billing support with the order number and the last four digits of the card.
3. After we verify the duplicate, the extra charge is refunded within **5 business days**, plus the 3 to 10 business days your bank needs to post it.

## Unrecognized charges

If you see a Voltix charge you do not recognize, contact us before opening a dispute with your bank. Disputes can take up to 60 days to resolve, while we can usually explain or refund the charge much faster.

# Subscription Plans and Billing

Voltix offers two consumer subscriptions.

## Plans and prices

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly |
|---|---|---|
| Voltix Care+ (per device) | $9.99 | $99.00 |
| Voltix Cloud Backup 1 TB | $5.99 | $59.00 |
| Voltix Cloud Backup 5 TB | $19.99 | $199.00 |

## Renewal

Subscriptions **renew automatically** at the end of each billing period, using the default payment method. We email a reminder **7 days before** a yearly renewal.

## Upgrades and downgrades

- **Upgrades** (for example, 1 TB to 5 TB) take effect immediately. You are charged the **prorated difference** for the rest of the current period.
- **Downgrades** take effect at the **next renewal**. Make sure your stored data fits the smaller plan before the renewal date.

## Switching from monthly to yearly

You can switch to yearly billing at any time. The yearly plan starts at the end of your current monthly period.

For failed renewals, see "Failed Payments and Declined Cards". To stop a subscription, see "Cancelling a Subscription".

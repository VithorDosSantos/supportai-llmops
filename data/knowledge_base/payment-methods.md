# Accepted Payment Methods

## Personal accounts

Voltix accepts the following payment methods for online orders and subscriptions:

- Visa, Mastercard and American Express credit or debit cards;
- PayPal;
- Apple Pay and Google Pay (on supported devices and browsers).

Cards are charged when the order **ships**. At checkout we only place an authorization hold for the order amount.

## Business accounts

Voltix Business accounts can also pay by **bank transfer against an invoice** with **net-30** terms. Invoice payment must be enabled by the account owner in **Admin > Billing**, and it is subject to a credit check.

## Currency

All prices are shown and charged in **US dollars (USD)**.

## Saved cards

You can save up to five cards in **Account > Payment methods**. Card details are stored by our payment processor; Voltix never stores your full card number.

# Integrations and Compatibility

## Voltix smart-home devices

Voltix smart-home devices work with **Amazon Alexa**, **Google Home** and **Apple Home**. Link them in the Voltix app under **Settings > Integrations**.

## Voltix Business integrations

Voltix Business provides built-in integrations with:

- **Slack** and **Microsoft Teams** for incident and device alerts;
- **Jira** for creating issues from support tickets;
- **Google Workspace** and **Microsoft Entra ID** for user directories (see "Single Sign-On for Voltix Business").

Integrations are enabled by an administrator in **Admin > Integrations**. Each integration can be limited to specific teams.

## Webhooks and custom integrations

For tools without a built-in integration, use **webhooks** (Admin > Integrations > Webhooks) or the Voltix Business API (see "API Access and Rate Limits").

## Peripherals

Voltix laptops support USB-C docks that follow the USB4 or Thunderbolt 4 standards. Older docks may work with limited features.

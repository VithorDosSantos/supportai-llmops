# Cancelling a Subscription

You can cancel Voltix Care+ or Voltix Cloud Backup at any time from **Account > Subscriptions > Cancel**.

## What happens after cancelling

- Your subscription stays active until the **end of the current billing period**. It will not renew.
- For Cloud Backup, your files remain available until the end of the period and are then kept for **30 days** before permanent deletion. Download anything you need during that time.
- For Care+, coverage ends at the end of the period. Claims opened before that date are still handled.

## Refunds for cancelled subscriptions

- **Monthly plans:** no refunds for the current month.
- **Yearly plans:** full refund if you cancel within **14 days** of the purchase or of the yearly renewal. After 14 days, yearly plans are **not refunded**, not even partially.

## Reactivating

You can reactivate a cancelled subscription before it ends without losing data or coverage.

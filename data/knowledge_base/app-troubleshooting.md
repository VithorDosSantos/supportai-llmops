# Voltix App Crashes and Errors

The Voltix app is available for iOS 16+, Android 11+, Windows 10+ and macOS 13+.

## App crashes or freezes

1. Update the app to the latest version.
2. Restart your phone or computer.
3. Clear the app cache: **Settings > Storage > Clear cache**. This does not delete your account data.
4. Uninstall and reinstall the app.

## "Unable to sync" error

- Check that the device has internet access.
- Sign out and sign in again.
- Check the service status page (see "Service Status and Maintenance") in case of an ongoing incident.

## Sending diagnostics

If the problem continues, go to **Settings > Help > Send diagnostics** and include the diagnostics ID in your ticket. Diagnostics contain app logs and device model, but **no passwords or personal files**.

# Warranty Coverage

## Standard warranty

Voltix-branded devices come with a **1-year limited warranty** from the delivery date. Products of other brands sold by Voltix are covered by their **manufacturer's warranty**; we help you contact the manufacturer.

## What is covered

- Defects in materials and workmanship under normal use.
- Battery capacity below **80%** of the original within the warranty period.

## What is not covered

- Accidental damage, such as drops, cracked screens and liquid damage.
- Damage caused by unauthorized repairs or modifications.
- Normal wear, such as scratches on the case.
- Data stored on the device.

## Voltix Care+

Voltix Care+ extends coverage to **3 years in total** and adds **accidental damage** protection:

- up to **2 accidental damage claims per year**;
- a **$49 service fee** per accidental damage claim;
- no service fee for defects.

Care+ must be purchased within **30 days** of buying the device. To file a claim, see "Warranty Claim Process".

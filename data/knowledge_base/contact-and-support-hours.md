# Contact Channels and Support Hours

## Channels

- **Chat:** in the Voltix app and on the website, during support hours.
- **Email / ticket:** support@voltix.example, any time. You get an automatic ticket number.
- **Phone:** available for Voltix Care+ members and Voltix Business customers.

## Hours (Eastern Time)

- Monday to Friday: 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
- Saturday: 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
- Sunday and US public holidays: closed. Email tickets are answered on the next business day.

Voltix Business customers can report **critical incidents 24/7** through the Admin console.

## Response time targets

| Ticket priority | First response |
|---|---|
| High | within 4 business hours |
| Medium | within 1 business day |
| Low | within 3 business days |

Priority is set by our support team based on the impact described in the ticket. Please send one ticket per issue; duplicated tickets slow down the response.

# Tracking Your Order

## Order statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Received | We received the order and the payment authorization. |
| Preparing | The order is being picked and packed. It can no longer be changed. |
| Shipped | The package was handed to the carrier. |
| Delivered | The carrier confirmed delivery. |

## Tracking number

You receive an email with the tracking number within **24 hours** after the order ships. The tracking number is also shown in **Account > Orders**.

Right after shipping, the carrier may show only "Label created" for **24 to 48 hours**. This is normal.

## Package not moving or not delivered

- If tracking shows **no movement for 5 business days**, contact support with your order number.
- If tracking says "Delivered" but you cannot find the package, check with neighbors and your building reception, and wait until the end of the next business day. Then contact support.

We open an investigation with the carrier, which takes up to **7 business days**. If the package is confirmed lost, we ship a replacement or issue a full refund, whichever you prefer.

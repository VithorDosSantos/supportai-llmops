# Exchange Policy

An exchange replaces an item you bought with a different variant (color, storage size, configuration) or with a different product. Exchanges follow the same **30-day window** as returns (45 days for Voltix Care+ members).

## How exchanges differ from returns

- With an **exchange** you receive another item; no money goes back to you unless the new item is cheaper.
- With a **return** you send the item back and receive a refund (see "Refund Policy").

## Price differences

- If the new item costs **more**, the difference is charged to your original payment method when the exchange is confirmed.
- If the new item costs **less**, the difference is refunded to your original payment method after we receive the original item.

## Shipping of the replacement

The replacement ships as soon as the carrier **scans your return package**, so you do not have to wait for it to reach our warehouse. Exchanges ship free with standard shipping.

## Limits

- Each item can be exchanged **only once**. A second change must be handled as a return and a new order.
- If the requested replacement is out of stock, the exchange is automatically converted into a return and refunded.
- The restocking fee does not apply to exchanges for the same product in a different variant.

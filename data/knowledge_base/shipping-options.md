# Shipping Options and Delivery Times

| Option | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3 to 5 business days | Free on orders over $50, otherwise $4.99 |
| Express | 1 to 2 business days | $14.99 |
| Next Day | Next business day | $24.99 |

Delivery times start when the order ships, not when it is placed. Orders placed before **2 p.m. (local time of the delivery address)** on a business day usually ship the same day.

## Large items

Monitors larger than 32 inches and desktop workstations ship with Standard shipping only.

## Split shipments

If items in one order come from different warehouses, they may arrive in separate packages. You pay shipping only once.

## Delivery signature

Orders above **$500** require a signature on delivery. If nobody is home, the carrier leaves a notice and tries again on the next business day.

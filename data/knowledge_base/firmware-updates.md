# Firmware Updates

## Automatic updates

Voltix devices install firmware updates automatically **every night between 2:00 and 4:00 a.m.** (local time of the device), when an update is available. The device may restart during this window.

## Manual updates

To update immediately, open the Voltix app, select the device and tap **Check for updates**.

## During an update

- **Do not unplug** the device or close the app until the update finishes. Updates usually take 5 to 15 minutes.
- The status light pulses blue during the update.

## Problems after an update

If a device stops working after an update, restart it once. If it still does not work, contact Technical Support. Rolling back to an older firmware version **cannot be done by customers**; only support can do it in specific cases.

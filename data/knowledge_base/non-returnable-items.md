# Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned or exchanged unless they arrive damaged or defective:

- **Software and digital licenses** once the license key has been revealed or activated.
- **Personalized items**, such as engraved devices or custom-built configurations.
- **Opened in-ear headphones and earbuds**, for hygiene reasons.
- **Opened consumables**, such as printer ink and toner cartridges.
- Items marked **Final Sale** on the product page.
- **Subscriptions** such as Voltix Care+ and Voltix Cloud Backup. These are cancelled, not returned (see "Cancelling a Subscription").

Defective items from this list are still covered by the warranty (see "Warranty Coverage").

# Invoices and Receipts

## Downloading an invoice

Invoices are created when the order ships. To download one:

1. Go to **Account > Orders**.
2. Open the order and click **Invoice (PDF)**.

Subscription invoices are in **Account > Subscriptions > Billing history**.

## Changing invoice details

You can edit the **company name, billing address and tax ID** shown on an invoice for up to **30 days after the invoice date**. Open the invoice and click **Edit billing details**; a corrected invoice replaces the original. After 30 days, invoices can no longer be changed.

## Receipts for split shipments

If an order ships in several packages, you get one invoice per package. The amounts add up to the order total.

## Business accounts

Voltix Business administrators can download all invoices of the organization from **Admin > Billing > Invoices**, including invoices paid by bank transfer.

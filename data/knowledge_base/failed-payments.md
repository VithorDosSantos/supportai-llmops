# Failed Payments and Declined Cards

## Declined at checkout

Common reasons for a declined card:

- the billing address does not match the address registered with your bank;
- the card has expired or the security code is wrong;
- your bank blocked the transaction as a fraud precaution. Approving it in your banking app and trying again usually fixes it;
- insufficient funds or credit limit.

Voltix cannot see the exact reason for a decline. Only your bank can tell you.

## Failed subscription renewals

If a renewal payment fails:

1. We **retry the payment 3 times over 7 days** and email you after each attempt.
2. If all retries fail, the subscription is **paused**: Care+ coverage is suspended and Cloud Backup becomes read-only.
3. Update your card in **Account > Payment methods** to resume the subscription immediately. The outstanding amount is charged at that moment.

Paused subscriptions that are not paid within **30 days** are cancelled. For Cloud Backup, the data deletion rules in "Cancelling a Subscription" then apply.

# Warranty Claim Process

Use this process for devices that stopped working **after** the first 48 hours. Problems found on arrival follow "Damaged on Arrival or Wrong Item Received".

## Before you start

- Try the troubleshooting steps for your device.
- Back up your data. Repairs may erase the device.
- Find the **serial number** (on the device label or in the Voltix app under **Device > About**).

## Opening a claim

1. Go to **Account > Devices** and select the device. If the device is not listed, register it first (see "Product Registration").
2. Click **Start a warranty claim** and describe the problem.
3. Print the prepaid shipping label and send the device **without accessories**.

## After we receive the device

- Diagnosis takes **3 to 5 business days**.
- If the defect is covered, we **repair or replace** the device. Replacements may be new or certified refurbished units with the same specifications.
- Repaired or replaced devices keep the remaining warranty period, or **90 days**, whichever is longer.

If the problem is not covered, we send a repair quote. If you decline it, the device is returned to you at no cost.

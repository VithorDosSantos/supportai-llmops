# Setting Up a New Voltix Device

## Before you begin

- Install the Voltix app and sign in.
- Have your Wi-Fi network name and password ready.
- Charge battery-powered devices for at least **30 minutes**.

## Setup steps

1. Power on the device. The status light blinks white when it is ready to pair.
2. In the app, tap **Add device** and scan the QR code on the device or on the box.
3. Choose your Wi-Fi network. Voltix smart-home devices only support **2.4 GHz** networks during setup.
4. Wait for the firmware check. The device may update before it is ready to use.

## Laptops and desktops

Voltix laptops and desktops come with the operating system preinstalled. On first start, follow the on-screen setup and then install the Voltix app to register the device and receive driver updates.

## Setup fails

If pairing fails, restart the device and the phone, and keep them within 3 meters of each other. See "Troubleshooting Wi-Fi and Network Connectivity" for network problems.

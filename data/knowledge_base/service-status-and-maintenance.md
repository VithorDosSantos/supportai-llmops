# Service Status and Maintenance

## Status page

The current status of Voltix online services (website, app sync, Cloud Backup, Voltix Business) is published at **status.voltix.example**. You can subscribe to email or SMS notifications for incidents.

## Planned maintenance

Planned maintenance happens on **Sundays between 2:00 and 4:00 a.m. UTC**. We announce it on the status page at least **72 hours** in advance. During maintenance, some services may be slow or unavailable for a few minutes.

## During an outage

- There is no need to open a ticket for an incident already listed on the status page; updates are posted there every 30 minutes.
- Cloud Backup automatically resumes after the outage; no data is lost.

## Uptime commitment for Voltix Business

Voltix Business includes a **99.9% monthly uptime** commitment. If uptime is below the commitment, administrators can request a service credit within 30 days after the end of the month:

| Monthly uptime | Credit |
|---|---|
| below 99.9% | 10% of the monthly fee |
| below 99.0% | 25% of the monthly fee |

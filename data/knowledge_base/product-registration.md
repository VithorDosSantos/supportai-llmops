# Product Registration

Registering a device links it to your account so you can open warranty claims, receive firmware notices and buy Voltix Care+.

## Automatic registration

Devices bought from voltix.example while signed in are **registered automatically**. Devices set up through the Voltix app are also registered during setup.

## Manual registration

For devices bought from another retailer or received as a gift:

1. Go to **Account > Devices > Register a device**.
2. Enter the **serial number** and the **purchase date**.
3. Upload the proof of purchase (receipt or invoice).

Registration is confirmed within **2 business days**.

## Transferring a device

If you sell or give away a device, remove it from **Account > Devices** first. The remaining warranty transfers to the new owner, who registers it with the original proof of purchase. Voltix Care+ does **not** transfer.

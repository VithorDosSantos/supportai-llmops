# Damaged on Arrival or Wrong Item Received

If your package arrives damaged, the device does not work out of the box, or you received a different item than you ordered, we handle it with priority.

## Report within 48 hours

Report the problem within **48 hours of delivery** from **Account > Orders > Report a problem**. Include:

- photos of the item and of the shipping box, including the shipping label;
- a short description of the damage or of the item you received.

Reports made after 48 hours are handled as a regular return or as a warranty claim.

## What we do

- We ship a **free express replacement** right away, without waiting for the original item to come back.
- You receive a prepaid label to send the damaged or wrong item back within 14 days.
- No restocking fee and no return shipping fee apply.

If the item is out of stock, we issue a full refund instead, including the original shipping cost.

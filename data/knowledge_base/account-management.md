# Managing Your Voltix Account

## Resetting your password

Click **Forgot password** on the sign-in page. The reset link is valid for **30 minutes**. If it expired, request a new one.

## Changing your email address

Go to **Account > Profile > Email**. We send a confirmation link to **both** the old and the new address; the change only takes effect after both are confirmed.

## Two-factor authentication

We recommend enabling two-factor authentication in **Account > Security**. See "Security and Data Protection" for the supported methods.

## Deleting your account

You can request account deletion in **Account > Privacy > Delete account**.

- Active subscriptions must be cancelled first.
- Deletion is completed within **30 days** of the request.
- Order and invoice records are kept for **5 years**, as required for tax purposes, even after the account is deleted.

Deleted accounts cannot be restored.
